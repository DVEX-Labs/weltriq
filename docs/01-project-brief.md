# WELTRIQ Project Brief

## Overview
**Brand**: WELTRIQ
**Tagline**: DEFINE • DESIGN • DELIVER
**Positioning**: Kerala's Trusted Roofing Solutions Partner
**Primary Goal**: Build a premium digital presence that communicates professionalism, trust, structured project execution, quality control, and accountability. It is a conversion-focused company website designed to generate qualified roofing enquiries.

## Target Audience
- Residential projects
- Commercial projects
- Industrial projects
(Initial primary focus: Residential Roofing Projects in Kerala)

## Core Value Proposition
"Professionally managed roofing, with one accountable partner from planning to completion."

## Tone & Vibe
- Premium, architectural, engineering-focused
- Professional, modern, clean, confident
- Minimal visual noise, strong hierarchy, restrained motion
- Highly trustworthy and authoritative
- Must NOT feel like a generic local contractor template or a SaaS dashboard

## Key Objectives
1. **Explain the Service**: What WELTRIQ does and who it serves.
2. **Differentiate**: Why the organized, project-delivery approach is superior to traditional unorganized contractors.
3. **Detail the Process**: How the 8-step roofing process works.
4. **Build Trust**: Professionalism, accountability, guarantees (24h inspection, 48h quotation).
5. **Convert**: Drive users to contact/enquire via form or WhatsApp.
