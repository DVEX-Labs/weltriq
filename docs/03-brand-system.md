# Brand System

## Visual Identity
The visual identity must feel architectural, premium, technical, confident, clean, professional, and modern. 

## Typography
- **Primary / Display Font**: Bakbak One (Used for strong brand impact)
- **Secondary / Body Font**: Garet (Used for readable supporting content)

## Color Palette
**Primary Colors**:
- Sunshine Yellow (Accent color)
- Dark Blue / Navy (Premium sections and contrast)
- Black
- White

**Secondary Colors**:
- Sunshine Yellow
- Island Spice
- Black
- White

*Rule*: Extract exact hex codes from the WELTRIQ Brand Kit PDF. Do not approximate. Yellow should act as a subtle accent, not overwhelm the UI.

## Logo
- Must follow the official WELTRIQ brand kit.
- Do NOT modify proportions, colors, geometry, or lockup.
